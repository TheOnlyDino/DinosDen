4/28/2026      uncertainty
History is my passion. Whether it be researching family history, transcribing historical documents, or my very particular interest in Afro-Indigenous relations in Indian Territory (primarily modern Oklahoma, United States). I love it all. However, I have no clue what to do with this interest. For a long time I wanted to be a genealogist, but wasn't able to build up a large enough portfolio. I also really wanted to work in museums, and I currently do, but I'm not doing the kind of research I would like to do. I don't want to work in academia, so I am left with few options out there.
Oh well, I am sure future me will figure something out. For now, I think it would be fun to dedicate future portions of this site to historical happenings?

4/27/2026      writing a paper
I am taking a course on America in the 1960s and I like to learn about queer history so I thought "I'll write a paper on contemporary news coverage of Stonewall!"

I am now regretting that decision

Finding sources for this paper has been such a struggle, whether it be due to copyright or the source only existing in one place somewhere far off. I have been waiting on a scan of The East Village Other, an underground counter-culture newspaper, since February and this paper is due in a week!
I must be doing something right if this is so challenging? Idk.
